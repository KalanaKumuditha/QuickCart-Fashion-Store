# IT24104345 – User Engagement & Recommendation

## Assigned Functionalities
* Monitor customer behavior
* Generate personalized product suggestions
* Suggest related items through “Frequently Bought Together” logic
* Display a recently explored products section
* Implement automated reminders for incomplete (abandoned) carts
* Categorize product

## Codebase Attribution & Methods

### `com.app.store.service.RecommendationService.java`
* `trackProductView()` - Monitors customer behavior by saving timestamps to the `BrowsingHistory` entity database every time a product is clicked.
* `getRecentlyExploredProducts()` - Queries the user's history to display their recently explored section on the homepage.
* `getFrequentlyBoughtTogether()` - Executes heavy SQL grouping arrays to find related items based on historical shared-cart checkouts.
* `getPersonalizedSuggestions()` - Analyzes user preferences to generate personalized product suggestions.

### `com.app.store.service.impl.AbandonedCartService.java`
* `checkAbandonedCarts()` - A `@Scheduled` background worker that runs autonomously to identify incomplete carts older than a specified duration.
* Integrates with `EmailService.sendAbandonedCartReminder()` to dispatch automated followup reminders.

### `com.app.store.controller.AdminCategoryController.java` & `CategoryController.java`
* Exposes the architecture allowing admins to strictly categorize products using formal `Category` entities, replacing free-form text input and providing dynamic collections for shoppers.

### Frontend & UI Templates
* `templates/index.html` - Renders the asynchronous "Recently Explored Products" horizontal carousel section based on behavior tracking.
* `templates/product-details.html` - Appends the personalized algorithmic "Frequently Bought Together" suggestion loops beneath the primary product card.
* `templates/category/list.html` & `templates/category/detail.html` - The global categorization storefronts natively isolating product catalogs.

## Workflow Explanation
The User Engagement loop operates autonomously in the background to drive retention. As users navigate the storefront, `RecommendationService.trackProductView()` silently logs their product interactions. This intelligence is immediately used by `getRecentlyExploredProducts()` to personalize their homepage, and `getFrequentlyBoughtTogether()` to cross-sell mathematically relevant items on the detail pages. Additionally, the Admin Category subsystem ensures all inventory is strictly grouped, allowing the recommendation engine to isolate preference metrics by clothing department. If a user leaves the website with items sitting in their Cart, the Spring Boot `@Scheduled` trigger inside `AbandonedCartService` wakes up, identifies the stale session, and automatically commands the `EmailService` to dispatch a recovery email.
