# IT24101067 – Order and Supplier Management

## Assigned Functionalities
* Centralized order dashboard
* Order lifecycle workflow
* Return and refund
* Contact seller
* Supplier profiles and onboarding
* Supplier dashboard
* Low stock alert and perches order
* Supplier performance metrics

## Codebase Attribution & Methods

### `com.app.store.controller.SellerOrderController.java` & `com.app.store.service.OrderService.java`
* `getOrders()` - Develops the centralized order dashboard restricting views exclusively to items sold by the authenticated supplier.

### `com.app.store.service.impl.OrderLifecycleService.java`
* `updateOrderStatus()` - Drives the Order Lifecycle Workflow enabling suppliers to step items from PROCESSING -> SHIPPED -> OUT_FOR_DELIVERY -> DELIVERED.
* `requestReturnByUser()` & `processReturn()` - Controls the Return and Refund pipelines, transferring funds back and restocking inventory.

### `com.app.store.controller.SellerMessageController.java` & `MessageController.java`
* `viewInbox()`, `viewChat()`, `sendMessage()` - Connects directly into the Messaging subsystem enabling buyers to contact sellers, isolating the chat explicitly by supplier ID.

### `com.app.store.controller.AuthController.java` & `com.app.store.service.UserService.java`
* `register()`, `registerUser()` - Houses the "Register as Seller/Supplier" toggle handling Supplier profiles and onboarding to securely grant the `ROLE_SELLER` authority state.

### `com.app.store.controller.SellerDashboardController.java`
* `dashboard()` - Generates the comprehensive Supplier dashboard.
* Uses native injection to highlight Low stock alerts by querying `ProductVariantRepository` for stock dropping below defined danger thresholds.
* Automatically compiles Supplier performance metrics (Gross Revenue, Total Units Sold, Best Sellers) specifically scoped to the active seller's private `Product` catalog mapping.

### Frontend & UI Templates
* `templates/seller/dashboard.html` - The master hub exposing supplier performance metrics, gross revenue scales, and the low stock alerts table.
* `templates/seller/orders.html` - The centralized supplier order dashboard housing the order lifecycle workflow (Processing -> Shipped -> Delivered) and return mechanics.
* `templates/seller/inbox.html` & `templates/seller/chat.html` - The direct messaging interface satisfying the "Contact Seller" supplier-side dashboard block.
* `templates/user/messages.html` & `templates/user/chat.html` - The symmetric buyer-side direct layout to initiate contact with sellers.
* `templates/register.html` - Contains the "Register as Seller/Supplier" onboarding forms granting dashboard elevation.

## Workflow Explanation
The Order and Supplier Management workflow transforms Quick Cart into a multi-vendor platform. When a user requests Supplier onboarding during registration, `AuthController` tags their entity with `ROLE_SELLER`. They are granted exclusive access to the `SellerDashboardController` which computes their live performance metrics and low stock alerts. As customers purchase their products, the `OrderService` routes the specific line items into their centralized `SellerOrderController` dashboard. From there, the seller utilizes `OrderLifecycleService` to manually transition the shipping states. If a buyer needs assistance or wishes to initiate a return, they trigger the `MessageService` which opens a direct contact loop natively inside the seller's `SellerMessageController` inbox.
